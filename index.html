Instructions

HRV Resonance Breathing is slow, steady breathing that helps synchronize your breath with natural heart rhythm patterns (HRV). Most people resonate best around 6 breaths/min (about a 10-second cycle).

Choose a simple pattern (5/5, 4/6, 3/7, 2/8), keep the breath smooth and quiet, stay still, and let the rhythm settle. If you feel lightheaded, reduce depth or stop.
